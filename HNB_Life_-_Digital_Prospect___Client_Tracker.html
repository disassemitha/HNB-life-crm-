🏦 Standing Order vs. Cash Collection

Payment continuity guide and Sinhala scripts

🏦 Bank Standing Order

  • Scheduled automatic payment
  • Less manual follow-up for collections
  • Review account balance and mandate status
  • Confirm successful setup with the bank

💵 Cash Collection

  • Requires consistent reminders and coordination
  • Payment timing can vary
  • Record every receipt and reconcile promptly
  • Discuss convenient digital alternatives

Setup checklist

  1. Obtain the client's informed instruction and required policy/bank details.
  2. Use the bank's official app or branch to locate scheduled payments / standing orders.
  3. Enter insurer payee and reference exactly as confirmed by the bank or HNB Life.
  4. Review amount, frequency, start date and account details with the client before authorizing.
  5. Save confirmation and verify the first payment; contact the bank for SLIPS/CEFTS or other-bank setup guidance.

Commercial Bank, Sampath, BOC, People's Bank and other providers may have different menus and requirements. Follow current official bank instructions; never collect PINs or OTPs.
